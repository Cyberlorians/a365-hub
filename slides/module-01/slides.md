# Module 1: See It. Control It. Protect It.

Teaching reference for the seventeen-slide HTML deck. Introductory concepts and
read-only review stay inside this workshop; no external course, build, chat or
knowledge test is a prerequisite. Instructor budget: 12-minute onboarding plus
25-minute core, subject to rehearsal. Student labs have no duration labels.

## Slide 1 - Get your lab account

Request the approved event account. Follow each numbered lab when released, then
return to the teaching slides. Never use production credentials.

## Slide 2 - Use the approved access process

Open the released event form, provide only the requested details, accept the terms
and submit once. Lab 1 requests access, not agent creation.

## Slide 3 - Check the lab-access email

Find the username, Temporary Access Pass and sign-in link. Read the actual lifetime;
keep credentials private. Check Junk and ask for help rather than resubmitting.

## Slide 4 - Sign in using the approved method

Open [Microsoft 365 admin center](https://admin.microsoft.com/) in a private window
with the lab account. Lab 2 only confirms that account. Do not bypass security prompts.

## Slide 5 - Confirm readiness without extra privileges

Use the approved account and standing read-only AI Reader for registry review.
No maker access, deployment, chat channel or knowledge test is required.
If access fails, use the authorized instructor live view and report the access gap.

## Slide 6 - See It. Control It. Protect It.

See purpose and accountable people. Control data and actions. Protect useful work
with evidence and response. Start with a useful employee task, not portal complexity.

## Slide 7 - What does an agent do?

An illustrative IT support task: help an employee find approved support guidance
and escalate what cannot be verified. Purpose, instructions, knowledge and tools
differ. Knowledge informs; tools perform configured actions. Instructions do not
replace permissions. This is a generic illustration, not a deployed sample claim.

Follow **Read > Decide > Act**, then compare two causes of the same misdirected
ticket: misused identity/management access versus misleading content. A valid
sign-in does not prove safe behavior. Compare sign-ins and changed grants with
the actual input, tool call and result; no attack or tool call is executed here.

## Slide 8 - Four questions for every agent

| Pillar | Question |
|---|---|
| Management | What exists, what does it do, and who owns it? |
| Governance | Who approves its access, changes and lifecycle? |
| Security | What data and actions are within bounds, and how are problems handled? |
| Business value | What useful outcome would justify it, and how will it be measured? |

The four authorized source screenshots are available in the expandable visual
reference on this slide. No other course is required; screenshot controls are
not interactive. The original icon illustrations keep the teaching view concise.

## Slide 9 - What does observability mean?

[Foundry observability](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/foundry/concepts/observability)
introduces evaluation (assess quality and safety), monitoring (watch behavior over
time) and tracing (follow execution steps). These are conceptual lenses here, not
a Foundry setup lab or guarantees of Copilot Studio/Agent 365 telemetry.

## Slide 10 - One job. Four Microsoft services.

Agent 365 helps review supported inventory and accountability; Entra addresses
identity and access; Purview addresses supported data/compliance controls; Defender
supports threat investigation and response. Each has its own coverage, roles and
configuration. No single portal proves all four pillars.

## Slide 11 - Review one prepared agent

Launch Lab 3 after this slide, then return to slide 12. Review the existing
**Project Beacon Relay Agent**, not a newly deployed helpdesk sample.
The six steps are find, owner, reach, staged risk, evidence and oral explanation.
Use admin.microsoft.com > Agents > All agents > Registry > Total agents, search
**Project Beacon Relay**, press **Enter**, then open **Project Beacon Relay Agent**.
No copying, worksheet, written submission or student changes.

## Slide 12 - Know who is accountable

Five Entra control areas connect the support task to its boundary:
identity (Entra Agent ID), accountability (Identity Governance), permissions
(entitlement management), sign-in conditions (Conditional Access/ID Protection)
and traffic boundary (Global Secure Access). Ask who acts, who manages it, why
access was approved, which rules apply and whether traffic follows a supported route.
This is a teaching map, not five setup tasks or a promise of universal coverage.

Read Details **Owner** and **Created by** on that same record. Distinguish ownership,
creation and approval; missing reporting is a gap, not proof nobody is accountable.
Name who should confirm the missing context.

## Slide 13 - Keep data and actions within bounds

Inspect **Data & tools** and **Permissions**. Blank or unavailable fields mean
unknown, not no access. Reported permissions alone do not prove effective access,
approval or enforcement. No grants, blocking or policy changes.

## Slide 14 - Know where to look for evidence

Connect evidence to a decision: **Spot it > Find who > Investigate > Contain >
Check it worked > Fix the gap**. Use the same illustrative misdirected ticket:
identity misuse and misleading content need different evidence. Defender and the
security operations center help connect supported signals; no automatic correlation
or ISOC preview is required. An event, alert and incident are different things.
Containment belongs to the authorized response team, not Module 1 learners.
Expand the evidence-location reference on this slide as needed, without leaving
the workshop.

Inspect **Activity**. Inventory usage is not a full execution trace, security
investigation or workload audit trail. Entra sign-in/audit, Purview Audit and
Defender Incidents & alerts answer different questions with separate access.
No Foundry or Copilot Studio Test activity is required.

## Slide 15 - Useful work needs measurable value

For the illustrative support task, consider time saved, answer quality, safe
escalation and user outcomes. Set a baseline and an accountable measure; do not
infer business value from registration or staged risk.

## Slide 16 - Explain what you know and what is missing

Orally explain one observed fact, one gap and the accountable next check.
On the same Relay record, **Security > Risk details** must show **High** and
**The agent is compromised.** This is staged training, not a live attack.
Do not use the faulty Agents at risk count filter. If missing, use the instructor
live view or report unresolved access/setup; do not call the check complete.

## Slide 17 - Establish normal before investigating abnormal

belong. The current mail runtime/content remains unchanged and needs its own
preflight. No replacement sample or optional maker path is claimed deployed.
