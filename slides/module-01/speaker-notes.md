# Module 1 Speaker Notes

Internal budget: 12-minute onboarding plus 25-minute core. Rehearse the six-step
read-only Lab 3; content validation does not verify fresh learner access.

## Slide 1 - Get your lab account

**2 minutes, onboarding.** Smoke-test the actual access route. Never project TAPs.

## Slide 2 - Use the approved access process

**3 minutes.** Explain minimal collection and submit once. Confirm workflow success.

## Slide 3 - Check the lab-access email

**4 minutes.** Check username, actual TAP lifetime and route privately. Support late
delivery without duplicate accounts.

## Slide 4 - Sign in using the approved method

**1 minute.** Confirm the lab account. No CA/MFA/device-policy bypass.

## Slide 5 - Confirm readiness without extra privileges

**2 minutes.** AI Reader is read-only inventory access, not maker or broad admin.
No deployment, chat or knowledge test. Move access failures to a helper.

## Slide 6 - See It. Control It. Protect It.

**1 minute.** Start with useful work and purpose, people, boundaries and evidence.

## Slide 7 - What does an agent do?

**3 minutes.** Use generic illustrative IT support, not a deployed sample claim.
Knowledge informs; tools act; instructions do not replace permissions.
Walk Read > Decide > Act, then compare two causes of the same wrong ticket:
identity/management misuse versus misleading content. Which evidence distinguishes
them? Sign-in success alone cannot. No attacks or tool calls are executed; keep
credential/token mechanics and hands-on investigation for Modules 3 and 6-7.

## Slide 8 - Four questions for every agent

**1 minute.** Introduce management, governance, security and business value using
the authorized four-pillar grounding. Do not conflate its rights with Image 3.
The four original screenshots are expandable on slide 8. Use the icon-based
teaching map first; expanding the source visuals is optional, not another lesson.
Do not attribute the source course to Microsoft or promise its planned release.

## Slide 9 - What does observability mean?

**3 minutes.** Evaluation assesses quality/safety; monitoring watches over time;
tracing follows execution. Ground the concepts in Foundry observability, not
automatic Foundry/Copilot Studio/Agent 365 integration or a setup lab.

## Slide 10 - One job. Four Microsoft services.

**1 minute.** Agent 365 inventory, Entra identity/access, Purview data/compliance
and Defender investigation have distinct coverage and roles. No four-portal demo.

## Slide 11 - Review one prepared agent

**3 minutes.** Launch six-step Lab 3; continue within slide 12's budget. Find the
existing Project Beacon Relay Agent through Registry > Total agents > search
Project Beacon Relay > Enter. Review owner, reach, risk, Activity and discuss.
Same selected record throughout. Security > Risk details must show High /
The agent is compromised. Staged training, not a live attack. Never use the
faulty Agents at risk count filter. Missing risk is unresolved, not complete.

## Slide 12 - Know who is accountable

**5 minutes.** Return here after Lab 3. Distinguish Owner, Created by and approval.
Report missing metadata and name the owner of the next check. No new helpdesk
deployment, maker role changes or student writes.
Teach the five Entra control areas: identity, accountability, permissions,
sign-in conditions and traffic boundary. Use the support task to explain each
question. Registry metadata is a clue, not proof of approval, expiry, policy
results or inspected routing. Coverage depends on the agent and supported flow;
configuration belongs in Modules 3-5.

## Slide 13 - Keep data and actions within bounds

**1.5 minutes.** Data & tools/Permissions blanks mean unknown, not no access.
Reported rights do not prove approval or enforcement. Keep authority depth later.

## Slide 14 - Know where to look for evidence

**1 minute.** Activity is inventory usage, not full traces or security/audit proof.
Entra, Purview and Defender follow-up needs separate roles. No required Foundry
or Copilot Studio Test activity. Module 2 runtime logs are a different source.
Teach Spot it > Find who > Investigate > Contain > Check it worked > Fix the gap
using the same hypothetical wrong ticket. Compare identity evidence with runtime
behavior before diagnosing it. An event, alert and incident differ; Defender
integration is not automatic and the ISOC preview is not required. Containment is
an authorized-team task in Module 7, not a Module 1 student action.

## Slide 15 - Useful work needs measurable value

**1.5 minutes.** Ask how time saved, quality and safe escalation compare with a
baseline. A registry entry does not prove useful outcomes.

## Slide 16 - Explain what you know and what is missing

**3 minutes.** Ask orally for an observed fact, a gap and an accountable next check.
Use the discussion guide. No worksheet, copying or submission. Missing access
uses the instructor live view or remains an explicit unresolved issue.

## Slide 17 - Establish normal before investigating abnormal

**1 minute.** Carry the method into Module 2. Using/testing and any optional maker
build belong there; current mail runtime/content remains unchanged. Do not imply a
replacement sample or optional path is deployed. Fresh learner access and timing
still need independent rehearsal.
